## M2 demo script (for the owner)

Sign in as the admin. On **Students**, add two students by hand, then **Import** the rest from a synthetic CSV (the
preview shows what will be added before you commit). On **Teams**, create a team with both hand-made students and set
a lead. On **Discover**, select an event and press **A**: pick the team, keep the **Hackathon** template, review the
stage dates the event gives (edit one if you like), and **Assign**. Open the competition: tick a few tasks and watch
the progress bar and the health line move; set a stage's date to yesterday to see **Overdue**, block one to see
**Blocked**. Move it to **Participating**, then **Record outcome** — Winner, rank 1, a prize in INR, and a certificate
link — and see it on the board's Completed card and on each student's page under Competitions & outcomes. Finally,
**Admin → Users → Transfer ownership…**: from yourself to the second professor, read the preview (teams,
competitions, mentorships, and what stays as history), **Confirm**, and show that the competition's history, roster
and outcome are unchanged while its mentor is now the second professor.
