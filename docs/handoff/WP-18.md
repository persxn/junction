## What was built

- **STEP 0:** spec v1.1.16; R94 (skill targets and three coverage states) and R95 (team lifecycle) in §21.
- **Contracts** (`teams.ts`): statuses, mentor roles and coverage states with label maps, list item, detail
  (members, past members, mentors, past mentors, targets, coverage, `can`), inputs, `StudentTeam`, the `TEAM_`
  codes, `team.transfer_primary`, predicates `isTeamMentor` / `isTeamPrimaryMentor` with matrix rows.
  `StudentListItem.teamCount` (open memberships) feeds the picker's "Also in 2 teams".
- **Backend:** migration 0024 (`teams` + `name_key`, `team_members`, `team_mentors`, `team_skill_targets`,
  the five partial unique indexes and the two lookups); `domain/teams/coverage.ts` (R94, pure, table-tested);
  `modules/teams` (records, members, mentors, reader) and `routes/teams.ts`, every write through `withAudit`
  with R92's no-PII wording and a `version` check; `GET /professors`. WP-17's students repository fills
  `teamMentorUserIds` (open mentors of ACTIVE teams) and `StudentDetail.teams`.
- **UI** (`features/teams/`, lazy routes `/teams`, `/teams/new`, `/teams/$teamId`, `/teams/$teamId/edit`):
  - **Teams list:** dense table (name · members · primary mentor · coverage in words · status), search, status
    chips (Active default, Archived, Any status), My teams, J/K/Enter, skeleton/empty/error states, guide.
  - **Team page:** members with role, Lead badge (icon + word), "Inactive — not counted in coverage"; ARIA
    menu button per member (Edit role… dialog, Make lead / Stop being lead, Remove); remove is optimistic with
    Undo re-adding the same role and lead; add-member combobox over ACTIVE students; earlier members kept;
    coverage chips + legend + "Skills this team needs" editor; Competitions empty state (WP-19); mentors with
    add/end co-mentor and the **Hand over…** dialog with "Step away"; Archive/Unarchive with a read-only banner.
  - Controls the viewer may not use are **disabled with the reason in words** (`team-model.disabledReason`),
    never hidden. A 409 `TEAM_VERSION_CONFLICT` puts the page back and shows "Someone else changed this team —
    reload to see their changes" with Reload.
  - **Form** (create/edit): name, description, targets; on create, first members with role and one lead, and
    for an administrator the primary mentor ("Me" only when the admin also holds PROFESSOR).
  - **Student page:** the Teams section lists current teams (link, role, Lead, Archived) and earlier ones.
  - `lib/roving-rows.ts` is the shared J/K roving row model; `StudentTable` now uses it too.
- **Seed:** `seed:demo --students` adds 12 synthetic teams (one student in three, co-mentors, all three states).

## UI faults fixed (step 8 sweep)

1. The member typeahead showed results for the previous search term for 200 ms; Enter could pick a student
   the user was no longer looking for. It now answers only for the text in the field.
2. "No active student matches" was a disabled `role="option"` inside the listbox (a screen reader counted it
   as a choice); it is now a `role="status"` line outside the listbox.
3. `StudentTable` carried its own copy of the J/K roving logic; both tables now share `lib/roving-rows.ts`.
4. Raw-text scan extended to team statuses, mentor roles and coverage states, with every `<details>` opened so
   earlier members and mentors are scanned too — clean on the list, Team page and Student page.
5. Forced states checked: unknown team, failing list, slow list (announced skeleton), no teams, archive making
   the page read-only; a lead change with the shell proxy as the only transport (R78/F9); Tab trap, Esc and
   focus return in the hand-over dialog.

## Minor decisions

- The list shows Active teams by default; Archived and Any status are chips (archived teams are history).
- Targets are edited as one comma-separated field (`parseTargets` de-duplicates case-insensitively).
- New members are added without a role; the role is set from the member menu afterwards.
- "Edit details" for a viewer who may not edit is a disabled button (a link cannot be disabled).
